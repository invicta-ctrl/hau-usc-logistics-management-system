# Impeccable v3 direction reset

## Status

Accepted owner direction implemented as a front-end-only design preview.

Claude left no persisted v3 source in the recovered design worktree. The v3
source therefore begins as a verified mechanical copy of v2 and applies the
new direction in a dedicated final stylesheet plus small semantic control
changes. V2 remains intact.

## Direction contract

- Thesis: a calm institutional operations desk with decisive hierarchy.
- Own world: HAU-USC Logistics public portals and role-based workspaces.
- Story: choose the right portal, understand attention, then act confidently.
- First viewport: brand, current place, primary work, one clear next action.
- Form: bright paper and oxblood anchors in light mode; charcoal and warm
  oxblood in dark mode.
- Finish: restrained gold, crisp rules, purposeful motion, no generic spinners.

## What changed from v2

- Warm sand recedes into bright paper and softly tinted neutral ground.
- Serif display headings become a firmer offline sans display system; the
  institutional wordmark alone keeps a bookish face.
- Equal attention cards become an asymmetric operational field with one lead
  exception and quieter supporting measures.
- Public portal cards use irregular spans and a clear primary route.
- The menu, return control, theme switch, and loading treatment become explicit
  interaction signatures rather than generic icon buttons.
- Spacing increases around decisions while tables remain dense enough for work.

## Preserved core

- All 32 owner-required representative workflows, covered by the inherited
  registry's current 33 surface entries and their states.
- Public/internal separation and sanitized illustrative data only.
- Role, workspace, scope, queue, request-line routing, lending, release,
  inventory, event, account, audit, and owner-health concepts.
- No stock deduction at request submission; no invented Reports area.
- Existing focus management, keyboard routing, modal behavior, and vocabulary.

## Out of scope

Backend code, Workers, migrations, providers, authentication, production data,
deployment, release branches, and any production write.
