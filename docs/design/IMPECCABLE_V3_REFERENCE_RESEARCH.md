## Principles carried into v3

1. Remove noise before adding decoration. V3 uses fewer simultaneous borders,
   clearer grouping, and larger negative space around the current task.
2. Let typography and contrast establish the first reading order. Page titles,
   attention values, status, and actions now have visibly different ranks.
3. Keep navigation predictable even when its treatment is distinctive. The
   authenticated rail retains the established information architecture; only
   its hierarchy, active state, and control finish change.
4. Use motion to explain state. Menu lines become a close mark, the theme thumb
   changes celestial state, and route/loading progress shows direction.
5. Design mobile as a complete composition. The 320, 375, and 414 pixel views
   reduce chrome and stack content without removing status or quantity truth.
6. Treat accessibility as a visual-system constraint: contrast, focus,
   non-colour status labels, reduced motion, and target size remain visible.

## Explicit anti-copy boundary

The research was used to extract general principles. No source-specific layout,
illustration, animation, wording, logo, image, or component was reproduced.
HAU-USC’s oxblood/gold identity, operational vocabulary, workflows, and existing
surface registry remain authoritative.
