# Impeccable v4 direction - Operational Choreography / Route Console

Status: owner direction pinned for the v4 isolated preview.
Genre: **modern-minimal**.
Macrostructure: **Map / Diagram**.

## Selection provenance

The initial random seed output assigned candidate 5 but its ephemeral key was
not retained. Before acceptance, that incomplete provenance was replaced by a
deterministic replay using key `00000004`. The Impeccable seed command
corroborated candidate 5 for `surface` / `operate` with the default seven
candidates. The replay was degraded: no catalog challengers or QUALITY BAR
boards were available. The key, scope, mode, assigned index, and degraded state
are now recorded in this direction contract, the source FORM block, and
Hallmark preflight evidence.

## Direction contract

- **Thesis:** a route console that makes every logistics handoff legible.
- **Own world:** HAU-USC Logistics public portals and role-based workspaces.
- **Story:** enter through the right portal, follow the route, and act without
  losing custody truth.
- **First viewport:** route, current bank, urgent work, and one clear next
  action.
- **Form:** oxblood signal rails, gold active segments, warm paper, and
  charcoal after dark.
- **Finish:** fast state choreography, linked geometry, precise controls, and a
  complete reduced-motion path.
- **Pinned navigation:** N13 authenticated command pill and N5 public floating
  bar. The inherited v3 N3/N9 treatment is preserved as history, not the v4
  direction.
- **Pinned footer:** Ft5 statement close, led by “Every item moves with a
  record.” and a quiet privacy/acceptable-use link.

## What is built

V4 is a front-end-only, vanilla-module preview. It preserves the accepted v3
route vocabulary and registry shape while adding `styles/v4.css` as the final
direction layer. The registry parity check reports **33 routes and 53 state
variants** in both v3 and v4.

The authenticated shell reads as a route map:

- a dark oxblood rail carries a gold route segment, a route node, two navigation
  banks, role/scope controls, and the current route code;
- the N13 topbar keeps menu, route identity, command search, theme, notifications,
  and account in one compact command line;
- a progress rule and three nodes sit between the topbar and the work canvas;
- the main canvas uses crosshair rules, a page-head right-angle, and a connected
  attention band to show where the operator is in the handoff;
- tables remain semantic queues, with split detail on wide screens, a drawer at
  tablet widths, and a full-width push on phones.

The public shell is calmer and narrower:

- N5 is a floating bar with institutional marks, theme, and a return control;
- landing uses a three-step portal route and one primary route per card;
- intake, tracking, sign-in, application, lending, policy, and receipt states
  keep protected stock, evidence, roster, supplier, and audit internals out of
  public markup;
- Ft5 closes with the custody statement and quiet legal/privacy navigation.

## Inherited product constraints

The v4 direction cannot weaken the accepted product or v3 contracts:

- public portals and internal workspaces stay visibly separate;
- request submission does not reserve or deduct stock; acceptance/reservation
  is distinct from physical release;
- movement history remains append-only and status labels remain explicit;
- missing information remains “Not recorded” or “Not assessed,” never an
  invented zero;
- UI hiding is not authorization; illustrative controls cannot imply a new
  capability;
- all figures are sanitized and illustrative, and no live service is contacted;
- keyboard operation, visible focus, labelled statuses, 44px touch targets,
  200% zoom, no horizontal overflow at 320px, and reduced motion remain part of
  the design contract.

## Responsive route geometry

The same map is reflowed at 320, 375, 414, 768, 1024, and 1440 CSS pixels.
At <=1023px the rail moves off-canvas; at <=767px the topbar becomes a compact
three-column command line and public cards/portal steps stack. Non-essential
labels yield before queue data, quantities, status, and the primary action.

## Not in scope

V4 does not redesign application runtime code, add routes to the product,
change domain behavior, connect providers, write operational data, deploy,
merge, release, or alter production. It is a reviewable visual artifact only.
