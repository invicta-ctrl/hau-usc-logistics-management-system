# HAU-USC Logistics controlled roadmap to v1.0

Status: **CONTROLLED POST-v0.7 ROADMAP — NOT LAUNCH AUTHORITY**

This roadmap begins only after v0.7.0 passes every production gate. It cannot
be used to defer a broken core workflow, missing production evidence, required
privacy control, backup/rollback proof, or an owner-approved upcoming-event
configuration.

## Inputs and governance

Roadmap work is selected from:

- measured findings from controlled live operation;
- the approved next-major-event structure and readiness review;
- support, incident, accessibility, and operator feedback;
- privacy-safe aggregate operational metrics;
- separately accepted specifications for every behavior or architecture change.

Owner approval, server authorization, immutable history, ledger truth, data
minimization, staging/production separation, and rollback requirements continue
to apply. Optional work does not receive production authority from this file.

## Weekly sequence after v0.7.0 launch

| Window | Focus | Deliverable | Exit gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Stabilization and feedback capture | Redacted support/incident themes, task-completion findings, and a ranked improvement list | No unresolved critical incident; observations contain no secrets or personal-data exports |
| Week 2 | Feedback-driven refinements | Small accessibility, clarity, recovery, and operator-efficiency changes | Accepted bounded specs, staging proof, regression coverage, and rollback note |
| Week 3 | Next-major-event configuration | Reusable event-series/sub-event templates, deadlines, request windows, committee ownership, and readiness checklist based only on approved values | Owner-approved event source, dry-run preview, scope validation, and no guessed event data |
| Week 4 | Advanced analytics | Privacy-safe aggregate cycle-time, readiness, exception, and workload views | Metric definitions reconciled to authoritative records; small-cell/private leakage review passes |
| Week 5 | Advanced notifications | Optional reminders and escalation policies for approved deadlines and exceptions | Explicit recipients, consent/role rules, rate limits, deduplication, delivery audit, and opt-out/recovery proof |
| Week 6 | Optional stock-media evaluation | Provider-neutral feasibility and attribution/security decision record | No provider key or live integration without a new accepted specification; owner-uploaded governed assets remain the default |
| Week 7 | Maintenance and reporting | Asset maintenance scheduling, advanced exports, and operator report prototypes | Existing asset/ledger history remains authoritative; exports are scoped, redacted, and access controlled |
| Week 8 | v1 prioritization and freeze proposal | Evidence-backed v1 scope, sequencing, risk register, and release criteria | Owner adoption of the v1 specification; unresolved v0.7 defects remain defects, not roadmap items |

## Candidate improvements

- Improvements learned from controlled live operation and the usability backlog.
- Repeatable configuration for the next approved major event.
- Advanced aggregate analytics and operational reporting.
- Optional stock-media integrations after privacy, attribution, licensing,
  availability, rate-limit, and credential reviews.
- Advanced notifications with explicit audience and delivery governance.
- Nonessential role-safe customization, saved views, and preference controls.
- Optional SSO only after a separate identity architecture and recovery review.

## Explicitly excluded from deferral

The following must be working and evidenced in v0.7.0 rather than moved here:

- authentication, authorization, access lifecycle, and role-route denial;
- public request/lending privacy and tracking isolation;
- inventory classification, availability, reservation, and ledger integrity;
- procurement, receiving, release, lending, return, and correction workflows;
- evidence persistence/recovery, backup/rollback, monitoring, and incident paths;
- privacy/consent/support surfaces and production operator guides;
- approved upcoming-event data required for a truthful launch dashboard.

The earlier prototype-oriented roadmap is superseded by this production-era
sequence. Historical implementation evidence remains in repository history and
the phase handoffs.
