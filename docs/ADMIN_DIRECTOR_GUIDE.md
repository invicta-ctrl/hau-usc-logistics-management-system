# Administrator and Director guide

## Separate authority domains

Administrator and Director are distinct server-owned roles. Administrator system/reference
authority does not imply operational stock or release authority. Director operational
oversight does not imply protected configuration, identity, permission, or environment
authority. Shared-screen visibility never expands either role.

## Administrator control desk

Use Reference Administration to inspect the exception, warning, or pending review first,
then open the controlled domain:

- **Access Management:** account, permission, and scope proposals. Permission activation,
  role assignment, committee expansion, and cross-office routing require a distinct reviewer;
  self-expansion is prohibited.
- **Reference Data:** approved versioned values through controlled fields. Do not edit raw
  operational sources from the interface.
- **Link Registry:** canonical route and ownership proposals with expected revisions and
  separation of duties.
- **Audit & System:** read-only sync health, evidence, and exceptions. Protected configuration
  remains outside the client.

Roster-owned identity and memberships are read-only. Emergency access is revocation-only and
audited. Every proposal and review is reauthorized against current state.

## Director workspace

Start from active event series, leadership decisions, cross-workflow blockers, and ready-to-
release signals. Use the action map to open the existing shared workspace for the governed
next action:

- cross-committee requests;
- canvassing, budget, procurement, and receiving blockers;
- controlled release readiness;
- lending review, overdue, handoff, and return context; or
- inventory attention, reservations, and movement history.

The bounded Management & Access panel is explanatory. It does not grant permission, create a
configuration path, or substitute for Administrator review.

## Review guardrails

- Confirm the environment and current revision before approval.
- Keep requester, event, item, quantity, unit, committee, and status context visible.
- Do not use role color, client labels, email domain, or a visible button as authority proof.
- Do not self-approve access expansion or collapse operational and system duties.
- Preserve append-only audit, status, receipt, release, and ledger history.
