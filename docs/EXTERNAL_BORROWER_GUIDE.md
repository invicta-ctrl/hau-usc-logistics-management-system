# External Borrower Guide

The public Lending Center does not require a staff login.

1. Choose the borrower type and department, then provide the required identity,
   contact, item, quantity, purpose, and due-date information.
2. Read and accept the privacy and acceptable-use notices.
3. Submit once. Save the private tracking reference; do not share it publicly.
4. Track the application through review, Ready to Claim, handoff, due, and
   returned/completed states.
5. Bring the required approved identity evidence when staff requests it.

Submission does not reserve or hand over an item. Staff review current
eligibility, available-to-promise stock, quantity limits, and identity evidence.
Public receipts and errors exclude internal account, storage, provider, and
OAuth details. For correction or privacy support, use the contact shown in the
site notice rather than putting sensitive information in a public message.
