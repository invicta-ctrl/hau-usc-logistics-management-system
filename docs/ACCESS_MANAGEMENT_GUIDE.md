# Access Management Guide

Access Management is restricted to authorized Administrators and the System
Owner under server-owned capabilities.

- Create starter accounts only for an approved role and scope.
- Preview Access ID and policy changes before applying them.
- Use the required distinct reviewer for permission, role, or scope expansion;
  self-expansion is prohibited.
- Reset temporary passwords without copying them into tickets, logs, or Git.
- Disable or revoke accounts and revoke sessions when access ends.
- Use unlock only after confirming the actor and the reason for the lock.
- Review Access ID history and audit records after every correction.

Starter accounts remain unusable until their governed activation/password
workflow is completed. Unauthorized roles receive denial and cannot use UI
visibility to expand authority.
