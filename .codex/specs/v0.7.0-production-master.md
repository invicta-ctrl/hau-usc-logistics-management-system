# BRANCH CONSOLIDATION PRINCIPLE

The owner's intent is to bring all accepted and unique work from every relevant branch into `main` so that `main` becomes the complete baseline.

Do not blindly merge every branch.

Blindly merging stacked, superseded, duplicate, historical, experimental, or incompatible branches can reintroduce obsolete code and duplicate commits.

Instead:

```text
Inventory every branch and PR
→ preserve every branch head
→ prove containment or identify unique work
→ integrate only accepted unique work
→ verify the consolidated candidate
→ merge the final consolidation PR to main
→ tag v0.7.0
→ close superseded PRs with evidence
→ optionally delete fully preserved and contained branches
```

Every branch must receive one classification:

```text
CONTAINED
UNIQUE_ACCEPTED
SUPERSEDED
HISTORICAL_ONLY
CONFLICTING_OR_REJECTED
UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_REVIEW
```

Rules:

- `CONTAINED`: do not merge again; record containment proof.
- `UNIQUE_ACCEPTED`: integrate by merge, cherry-pick, or focused reconstruction with tests.
- `SUPERSEDED`: preserve and close; do not reintroduce.
- `HISTORICAL_ONLY`: preserve with tag/bundle; do not merge into product code.
- `CONFLICTING_OR_REJECTED`: preserve evidence and exclude with reason.
- `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_REVIEW`: do not delete or close until resolved.

Preserve commit history and unique work before any branch cleanup.

Do not force-update `main`.

Do not resolve conflicts by discarding unknown work.
