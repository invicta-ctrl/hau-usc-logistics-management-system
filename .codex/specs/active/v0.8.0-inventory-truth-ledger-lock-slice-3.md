## Governing invariants

- `on_hand` is the sum of posted signed Inventory ledger quantities.
- `reserved` is the nonnegative remainder of active reservations after accepted consumption.
- `available_to_promise = on_hand - reserved`.
- Reservations and demand submission do not decrement physical on-hand.
- Physical effects are explicit, atomic, append-only, authorized, privacy-safe, and retry-safe.
- Unknown or invalid historical data is classified or quarantined, never guessed or rewritten.
- D1 is structured operational authority; Google and presentation fallbacks are not Inventory authority.
